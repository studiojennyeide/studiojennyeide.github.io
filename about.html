I’m an architect based in Oslo. I studied architecture at CEU Cardenal Herrera University in Valencia, and finished my master’s in 2025 with Massó Gardens, a project that turns a former canning factory in Galicia into a botanical garden with senior housing.

I’m most interested in existing buildings and what they already hold: their history, their place and the people around them. A lot of my work is about reuse, and about letting architecture and landscape work together.
